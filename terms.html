End-user license agreement

Draft for review · Updated 24 September 2026 · Final effective date not yet assigned

This is a proposed agreement for Canal Marina's internal automation. It is published for review and has not yet been adopted as a binding agreement. Production features described below remain subject to completion and testing.

1. Purpose and scope

Canal Marina ("we," "us") maintains CanalMarinaOdAutomation (the "Integration") to support dispatch orders, service pricing, and invoicing for its operations. The Integration is intended for authorized personnel and company administrators, rather than public registration or sale as a standalone software service.

Customers or their representatives may submit service information and signatures through a designated Tally form. Submitting an order does not grant access to the Integration's administration, other customers' information, or the connected QuickBooks company.

2. Authorized use

Once these terms are adopted, authorized users will receive a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable permission to use the Integration for Canal Marina's legitimate business operations, subject to their assigned responsibilities. No ownership of the Integration or third-party software is transferred.

Only an administrator with authority over the relevant QuickBooks company may authorize its connection. Users must safeguard their accounts and may not share credentials, bypass access restrictions, interfere with service operation, or access records outside their authorization.

3. Orders, pricing, and invoices

The current pilot receives Tally submissions, stores supporting PDFs, validates service details, and calculates crane-service charges using predefined pricing rules. The intended production workflow will map validated records to existing QuickBooks customers, services, and tax settings; create and check invoices; attach the corresponding order PDF; and send invoices when authorized.

Users are responsible for providing accurate order details and having authority to submit information and signatures on behalf of others. Authorized personnel must review flagged or incomplete records, maintain correct pricing and tax settings, and resolve discrepancies before invoicing proceeds.

An automatically calculated amount or generated record does not remove the company's responsibility for the accuracy of its invoices, tax treatment, and accounting records. The Integration does not provide legal, tax, or accounting advice. These terms do not replace a customer's underlying service agreement or alter agreed service prices.

4. Third-party services

The Integration uses Tally for form submissions, Supabase for backend processing and storage, and Intuit QuickBooks Online for accounting. Access and availability also depend on those providers and the company's applicable subscriptions and permissions. Their services remain subject to their own terms.

QuickBooks access is granted through Intuit's authorization process. The Integration does not ask users to provide their Intuit password to Canal Marina. It is independently maintained for Canal Marina and is not operated by Intuit, Tally, Supabase, or GitHub. Provider names identify compatibility and do not imply endorsement.

5. Data and confidentiality

The Privacy Policy draft explains the data involved and the intended handling practices. Users must only submit information necessary for legitimate service and invoicing purposes. Do not place passwords, API secrets, payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive information in order descriptions, attachments, or support messages.

Authorized personnel must keep customer and business information confidential and use it only for their permitted duties.

6. Availability, changes, and errors

The Integration is in development and may be interrupted, changed, or temporarily unavailable. Provider outages, incomplete orders, expired authorization, or validation failures may delay processing. Users should report suspected errors and check the existing invoice/order status before repeating an action.

Canal Marina may restrict access or suspend processing to investigate errors, protect information, or maintain the Integration. A manual business process may be needed during an interruption. No uninterrupted-service or error-free-operation guarantee is made in this draft.

7. Ending access or disconnecting QuickBooks

Authorized personnel may contact the administrator below to request that the Integration be stopped or disconnected. A QuickBooks administrator may also manage or revoke the app connection through Intuit's connected-app controls.

Stopping access does not automatically delete invoices already created in QuickBooks, recall messages already sent, or erase records that must be retained for legitimate accounting or legal purposes. Data requests are handled as described in the Privacy Policy.

8. Adoption and updates

Canal Marina will identify an effective date and communicate the final terms to authorized users before treating this draft as an adopted agreement. Later revisions will display an updated date. Nothing in these terms is intended to remove rights or obligations that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
