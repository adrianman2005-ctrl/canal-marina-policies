Privacy policy

Draft for review · Updated 24 September 2026 · Final effective date not yet assigned

This draft describes the current pilot and intended production data handling. Final organizational details, retention arrangements, and production safeguards must be confirmed before adoption. It is not a claim that all planned features or security controls are already implemented.

1. What this policy covers

Canal Marina ("we," "us") is developing CanalMarinaOdAutomation to receive dispatch orders, calculate service charges, and support invoicing through QuickBooks Online. This policy concerns information processed through that Integration and visits to these public documentation pages.

The Integration is an internal business automation. It has no public account registration. A designated Tally form collects order information from staff, customers, or their representatives. The company's administrator authorizes QuickBooks access through Intuit.

2. Information involved

  • Dispatch orders: customer/company and representative names, vessel details, service dates, service types, start/end times, order identifiers, form responses, signatures, and supporting order PDFs.
  • Pricing and processing records: validation results, billable quantities, applicable rates, calculated tax and totals, review status, and document references.
  • QuickBooks information: the connected company identifier and authorization credentials. The intended invoice workflow will also use customer/contact details, service items, relevant tax and company settings, and invoice/attachment identifiers and status needed to perform and verify invoicing.
  • Operational information: processing timestamps, connection status, limited diagnostic identifiers, and information voluntarily supplied when requesting support.

Information comes from submitted forms, authorized users, and the connected QuickBooks account. The Integration does not require Intuit passwords or payment-card details. Please do not submit unrelated sensitive information.

3. Why information is used

The purpose is to document requested/performed services, validate orders, calculate charges, prepare and verify invoices, associate the correct supporting documents, prevent duplicate processing, maintain business records, and respond to support or correction requests.

QuickBooks data is intended to be used only for the Integration's accounting and operational functions. The Integration is not designed to sell personal data, build advertising profiles, or expose records to unrelated users. Authority and any required notices or permissions for collecting another person's information or signature must be established through the business process.

4. Services and recipients

TallyCollects submitted orders and signatures and provides the corresponding documents to the Integration. The order form uses CAPTCHA to help prevent automated abuse. Tally privacy information.
SupabaseHosts the Integration's database, backend functions, and private document storage. The current project is hosted in the United States, so submitted information may be processed outside Panama. Supabase privacy information.
Intuit QuickBooks OnlineProvides the authorized accounting connection. The intended production workflow will send the information needed to create invoices and attach supporting PDFs, and retrieve relevant accounting information to verify the result. Intuit privacy information.
GitHub PagesHosts these public policy pages, not the Integration's customer database or order PDFs. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub privacy information.

Authorized company personnel may access information for their work. Intended invoice recipients receive the invoice information needed for billing. Authorized development/support personnel may use technical tools, including AI-assisted tools, while maintaining or testing the Integration. Production access and disclosures to such tools must be restricted to authorized purposes; the planned automated invoice process does not call a generative AI model to determine charges.

Information may also need to be provided in response to a valid legal obligation. Provider access, hosting locations, and any cross-border processing must be addressed in the final operational arrangements. These pages do not authorize unrelated third-party use of QuickBooks data.

5. Automated calculations and human review

Crane-service pricing uses predefined rules based on submitted service details and the applicable customer/vessel rate. Invalid or incomplete information can be held for review. Contact us if you believe an order, price, signature, or invoice is incorrect; an authorized person can investigate and arrange any necessary correction.

6. Security and access

The current pilot uses HTTPS endpoints, restricted backend database access, private PDF storage, encrypted OAuth tokens, and validation of Tally webhook signatures and OAuth authorization state. Additional production security and recovery work is still being completed and tested.

No method of storage or transmission eliminates all risk. The final operational process must limit access to authorized people and services, manage credentials securely, and avoid unnecessary sensitive data in diagnostics. Please do not include secrets or unrestricted customer records in support messages.

7. Retention and deletion

Order and invoice records are needed for service administration, accounting, dispute resolution, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Connection credentials and temporary technical data serve different purposes and should not be kept longer than needed for those purposes.

Specific retention schedules and deletion procedures are being finalized for production. No automatic deletion deadline is promised by this draft. You may contact us to request information, correction, or deletion. Requests will be assessed against the records involved and applicable obligations; disconnecting QuickBooks does not itself delete existing accounting records or documents held by separate providers. Backup copies may remain until the relevant backup retention cycle expires.

8. Privacy requests

Contact admin@canalmarinapanama.com to ask about information concerning you or to request access, correction, cancellation/deletion, objection, or portability where applicable. We may need enough information to verify your identity and authority before disclosing or changing a record. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested through an appropriate channel.

You may also seek guidance from Panama's Autoridad Nacional de Transparencia y Acceso a la Información (ANTAI). This draft does not limit rights available under applicable law.

9. Cookies and visits to these pages

These static policy pages include no application login, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, embedded forms, or cookies set by our page code. GitHub's hosting security logs still apply. Tally's form, its CAPTCHA service, Intuit's authorization pages, and other provider websites may use their own cookies and technical information under their respective policies. The Integration's OAuth flow uses a short-lived security cookie to validate authorization.

10. Updates

The final policy will identify its effective date. Updates will be published here with a revised date, and material changes will be communicated through an appropriate business channel. Review drafts should not be represented as adopted final policies.
