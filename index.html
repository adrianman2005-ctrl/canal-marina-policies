About the integration

Tally collects dispatch orders and signed supporting documents. Supabase stores and validates the submissions and calculates crane-service pricing using predefined rules. The planned QuickBooks Online workflow will create and verify invoices and attach the corresponding order PDFs.

This is an internal business automation. These public pages do not provide account registration, a QuickBooks connection form, payment processing, or access to customer records.
